How to connect to the cluster and start jobs?

  1. Connect to the university VPN.
  2. Use the following command to connect via SSH: ssh yourcs@{{SLURM_LOGIN_NODE}}

How to get an account?

Faculty members

You likely already have an LDAP account. It is usually your u:account with a "cs" suffix. This account is used to log in to the cluster.

However, you have to be added to the list of "allowed" accounts (i.e., to the slurm database). Please write an email and include the following information, so we can add you:

  • Your CS account name.
  • Your research group(s).

Students

Please write an email with (at least) the following information:

  • Your u:account name.
  • A description why you need access (e.g., your project, supervisor, ...).

Faculty members without an LDAP account

Please ask your local technician whether you can get one ... and then either write us your LDAP account or your u:account name together with your research group.
